Beat This!

Bundled file: beat_this-wgsl.bin
SHA-256: 6365ae5ba2e42922772da01fbfd0bde8dd20b7362a679018e78a5f251fe8a967
Original model: CPJKU's Beat This! final0 checkpoint.
Project: https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this
License copy: Beat-This-LICENSE.txt (MIT)
Exact source:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CPJKU/beat_this/b95c8ab0c58c2d9fcfd40508ae8dffbc05ac4f5c/LICENSE

The upstream README explicitly licenses both the code and published model weights
under MIT. Its licensing section also notes that some training material carries
copyright or limited Creative Commons terms, whose relevance depends on the use:
https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this/blob/b95c8ab0c58c2d9fcfd40508ae8dffbc05ac4f5c/README.md#license

Folia's build/beat_this_wgsl/export_weights.py --f16 folds normalization constants,
rearranges tensors, stores matrix weights as fp16, and packs the tensors and their
manifest into one file. These are export transformations of the original weights.

HTDemucs

Bundled file: htdemucs-wgsl.bin
SHA-256: 27d5331a74a4e23b18172b3d59d0378e698fd79bc0d0fed3316da77e3a37d8d1
Original model: Meta's four-stem Hybrid Transformer Demucs (htdemucs).
Project: https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs
License copy: Demucs-LICENSE.txt (MIT)
Exact source:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/demucs/e976d93ecc3865e5757426930257e200846a520a/LICENSE

The manifest records the transformed ONNX upstream as
https://huggingface.co/itamiArika/htdemucs-int8-memory
Its model card and LICENSE identify the derivation from StemSplitio/htdemucs-onnx
(revision d54ed9eb60e258ea82131c6ee14578628816456a), then Meta Demucs. The model
card licenses its conversion and quantization products under MIT.
License copy: htdemucs-itamiArika-LICENSE.txt
Exact source:
https://huggingface.co/itamiArika/htdemucs-int8-memory/raw/86db1d28b1bdec60a082f4e745c490298847307c/LICENSE

StemSplit's model card declares MIT and credits its ONNX export, verification and
packaging. The corresponding export-tool license is also included to retain its
copyright notice; the Hugging Face model repository has no separate LICENSE file.
Model card:
https://huggingface.co/StemSplitio/htdemucs-onnx/blob/d54ed9eb60e258ea82131c6ee14578628816456a/README.md
License copy: StemSplit-demucs-onnx-LICENSE.txt (MIT)
Exact source:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StemSplit/demucs-onnx/85db5c80aba33f0f2bdf88034a4be6539feec85b/LICENSE

Folia's build/htdemucs_wgsl/export_weights.py --f16 reads the project's earlier
htdemucs.onnx intermediate (108644650 bytes, SHA-256
099b5be76c1f6922124d07f850250f39d1f33f254a0b8cc90f4ec0dfd0912329). It preserves
the graph's fp16/fp32 tensor storage, transposes Linear weights, splits transposed
convolutions into phases, and packs weights, embeddings, windows and a manifest.
The shipped packed file is identified by its own hash above, rather than by an
unversioned Hugging Face filename. Export scripts are included in the source tree.
